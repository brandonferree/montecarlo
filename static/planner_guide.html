Cashflow Portfolio Analysis

A plain-language guide to the inputs and what the results mean.

This tool projects how an investment portfolio might grow, supply income, or both — across many possible market futures. Instead of assuming one fixed rate of return, it runs thousands of simulated scenarios and shows the range of outcomes, so you can see not just the average result but how likely it is your money lasts.

How to think about it. You describe a starting balance, how long the plan runs, and any money going in (contributions) or coming out (distributions). The tool then simulates the ups and downs of markets and reports the typical outcome along with the good and bad cases.

The inputs, one by one

Portfolio basics (sidebar)

Initial Investment
The starting value of the portfolio today.
Time Horizon (years)
How many years the projection runs. Often this is the years until — and through — retirement.
Inflation Rate
How fast prices rise each year. All contribution and distribution amounts you enter are in today's dollars and are grown by this rate over time, so the projection reflects real-world purchasing power. 3% is a common long-term estimate.
Distribution Frequency
How often money is added or withdrawn during the year — Annual, Quarterly, or Monthly. Monthly is the most realistic for retirement spending.
Enter amounts as (Annual / Monthly)
Convenience setting for how you type contribution and distribution figures. "Monthly" simply multiplies by 12 behind the scenes.
Return Assumptions
How stocks ("Equity") and bonds ("Fixed Income") are expected to perform. The BCM 2026 CMAs preset uses Becker Capital's own 10-year forward-looking estimates; "Custom" lets your advisor enter different numbers.

Scenarios

You can compare up to three side-by-side — for example, three different spending levels or three different stock/bond mixes.

Equity %
The share of the portfolio in stocks; the rest is bonds. Higher equity means more growth potential but bigger swings.
Contribution Years & Contribution amount
If you're still saving, how many years you'll keep adding money and how much per year (today's dollars). Set years to 0 if you're no longer contributing.
Distribution amount
How much you'll withdraw for income each year (today's dollars), beginning after the contribution years end.
Glide path (rebalance at retirement)
Optional. When on, the portfolio shifts to a different (usually more conservative) stock/bond mix once retirement begins — common practice to reduce risk as you start drawing income.
Retirement Equity %
The stock percentage to use after the glide-path switch.

Note / Other Income (optional)

Income streams
One-time or multi-year cash inflows shared across all scenarios — a property sale, an inheritance, a note receivable, or similar. Enter the annual amount (today's dollars), the year it starts, and how many years it lasts (1 = a single lump sum).

How to read the results

Median path & percentile bands
The middle line is the typical outcome; the shaded band shows the range from weaker to stronger markets. A wide band means more uncertainty.
Probability of success / chance the money lasts
The share of simulated futures in which the portfolio did not run out over the horizon. Higher is better.
Ending values
The spread of final portfolio balances — what might be left to spend or leave behind.

Important

This tool is for education and illustration only. Results depend on the assumptions you enter and are not a guarantee of future outcomes or a recommendation of any strategy. Review your plan with your Becker Capital advisor before acting.
